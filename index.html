Business model

Start as a service. Build toward a platform.

The first Uebey model is deliberately simple: sell execution and clarity before adding software complexity. Revenue can expand with recurring care, hosting support, optimization and additional digital products.

01

Launch

A defined project with a defined scope: landing page, website or digital tool. Best for validating the offer and creating cash flow.

02

Grow

New sections, campaigns, integrations, experiments and conversion improvements after the first version is live.

03

Care

Optional recurring maintenance, small updates, monitoring and technical continuity for projects that need ongoing support.

Strategic rule: Uebey should not compete with low-cost website builders on software features. It should compete on speed, judgment, implementation and the ability to turn a vague idea into something live. The platform layer can come later, once repeated client needs reveal what is worth automating.
